# Ending matrix

The final cell is derived from two independent axes:

|  | Family aligned | Family fractured | Control lost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Strong survival | Ending 1 | Ending 2 | Ending 3 |
| Fragile survival | Ending 4 | Ending 5 | Ending 6 |
| Financial collapse | Ending 7 | Ending 8 | Ending 9 |

Reputation is context for approval and stakeholder reaction; it is not a third outcome axis. `docs/case01/11_EXPECTED_PATHS.md` contains decision histories that exercise all nine cells through the UI flow.
