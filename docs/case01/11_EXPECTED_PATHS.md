# Expected outcome paths

These are legitimate decision-history fixtures for the current runtime engine. The first three dossiers and questions are selected within their screen limits; evidence IDs are selected only after they are opened. Timing and priorities remain fixed through reassessment. The examples demonstrate the nine cells without injecting a final tier directly into state.

| Financial | Governance / Family | Period 1 treatment and key decisions |
| --- | --- | --- |

| Strong survival | Family fractured | Open HS-D, HS-A, HS-G; use D evidence; choose PD-C; TG-C with the deterministic resolver succeeding; prioritize Creditors, Suppliers, Customers. |
| Strong survival | Control lost | Open HS-D, HS-E, HS-G; choose PD-D; TG-C with the deterministic resolver succeeding; prioritize Customers, Creditors, Holiday Inventory. | Fragile survival | Family aligned | Open HS-C, HS-B, HS-G; choose PD-B; TG-A; prioritize Governance & Family, Customers, Holiday Inventory. |
| Fragile survival | Family fractured | Open HS-A, HS-B, HS-G; choose PD-A; TG-A; prioritize Customers, Holiday Inventory, Employees. |
| Fragile survival | Control lost | Open HS-A, HS-B, HS-G; choose PD-B; TG-A; prioritize Suppliers, Employees, Customers. The verified dossier makes the separate Houndstooth trigger possible; the pressure event lowers continuity while the cash buffer remains fragile rather than collapsed. |
