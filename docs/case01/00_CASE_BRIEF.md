# MEDIFIN Case 01 — Toy Kingdom Inc.

Canonical identity: `case01` / `toy-kingdom-anderson`.

Toy Kingdom Inc. is a simulated specialty retailer with approximately 1,700 stores. FY2026 revenue is $11.540bn, Adjusted EBITDA is $792m, operating earnings are $460m and net loss attributable is −$36m. The Anderson family owns 78% and a private-equity fund owns 22%. The $6.6bn 2015 LBO and Anderson governance facts are part of this fictional educational simulation; they are kept separate from historical source limitations.

The player moves through screens 0–12. A selection is draft state until Confirm. Confirm freezes it, applies one transition, records provenance/events once, and advances the screen. Screen 10 is a seven-update timeline. Screen 11 can change diagnosis, treatment, both, or neither for Period 2 without rewriting Period 1.

Player-facing cards contain neutral descriptions of actions and hypotheses. Builder metadata such as relationships, reputation adjustments, execution conditions and outcome calculations is never rendered before the final screen. Feedback appears only on screen 12 or a valid terminal state.

HS-G is a separate governance dossier. Its contract is fictional: measured EBITDA `< $752.4m` is the trigger threshold derived from 95% × $792m. Trigger, exercise and control loss are separate state transitions.
