# Evidence and diagnosis relationships

The source brief is an archive/reference. The normalized runtime evidence is in `app.js`. Each dossier has a neutral scope before confirmation and full content after it opens. CH1/CH2 answers are clues unless the player has the relevant verified dossier. BCTC is an independent public source: it can establish repeated financial facts without opening HS-C or HS-D private annotations.

Diagnosis relationships are per evidence and use `SUPPORTS`, `CONTRADICTS`, `CONTEXT` or `UNRELATED`. Reasoning uses these relationships; it does not compare a shared topic string. A contradictory or unrelated fact selected as support lowers reasoning quality. Evidence is capped at three IDs and duplicates are rejected.

CD-F requires HS-D plus one of HS-A, HS-B, HS-E or HS-F in the selected dossiers. HS-G can inform governance but is not a prerequisite for CD-F. CD-E is about entertainment/licensing and portfolio mix; traditional toy categories remain positive in the supplied data.
